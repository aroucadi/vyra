# VYRA Autonomous Biomechanical Engine — Experience Charter

## 1. Product Vision

**Vyra is the Autonomous Biomechanical Engine & Training OS** — an elite, high-precision athletic companion engineered for structured hypertrophy lifters and ambitious professionals. Vyra bridges pure sports science mathematics with zero-friction in-gym execution:

1. **Pure Biomechanical Precision**: Submaximal 1RM formulas, greedy plate loading, deterministic auto-progression, and structural balance symmetry.
2. **Instant In-Gym Execution**: 5-second set logging, 1-exercise focus, pre-filled historical loads, synthetic rest audio chimes, and Screen Wake Lock.
3. **Anatomical Transparency**: Vector SVG micro-torso spotlighting target muscle tension in Emerald and synergists in Cyan, paired with two kinetic anchors.
4. **Adaptive Equipment Agility**: Instant equipment substitution matching occupied gym stations across a 6,554-exercise catalogue without breaking workout flow.
5. **Basement Gym Offline Resilience**: Write-ahead draft journaling and mutation replay queues ensuring zero data loss in zero-connectivity gym floors.

### The North Star
> *"Walk onto the gym floor, know exactly what to load, execute with biomechanical precision in under 45 minutes, and leave with verified progressive overload."*

## 2. Experience Foundation

### 2.1 The Anchor: The Day & Specialized Workspaces
The atomic temporal unit of Vyra is **the Day** (`/today`). Rather than dumping dense menus or competing dashboards onto the user, Vyra organizes athlete execution into four focused, high-performance workspaces:
- **Today (`/today`)**: Daily readiness check-in, hero workout card, supplement stack drawer, and quiet timeline.
- **Program (`/program`)**: 16-week periodized blueprint, week strip calendar, and mesocycle progression.
- **Exercises (`/exercises`)**: Canonical 6,554-exercise OpenGym catalogue browser with instant multi-filter search.
- **You (`/you`)**: Anthropometric metrics, structural balance radar (`/posture`), progress analytics (`/progress`), and privacy controls.

### 2.2 The Three Feeling Words (Evaluative Rubric)
Every UI element, button, animation, and interaction is judged strictly against these three feeling words:
- **Calm**: 60-30-10 Obsidian dark surface hierarchy (`#0B0F17`), generous whitespace, quiet transitions, zero visual noise or neon screaming.
- **Capable**: Instant (<50ms) search, deterministic sports science math, pre-filled steppers, sub-5-second set logging, zero cognitive overhead.
- **Personal**: Biometric autoregulation adapting to sleep and recovery, individualized structural balance ratios, and intelligent substitution matching available gym hardware.

### 2.3 Anti-Goals (What Vyra Must Never Be)
- ❌ **Not a Dense Dashboard / Control Panel**: No unsegmented monolithic scrolls, no raw float telemetry, no competing primary buttons.
- ❌ **Not a Conversational AI Chatbot**: No walls of generative text or markdown chat bubbles on the gym floor; all intelligence parameterizes closed action cards.
- ❌ **Not a Clinical Medical Application**: Zero clinical medical jargon (e.g. no "pathology" or clinical underweight advisories); all guidance is framed in athletic performance, joint preservation, and lean mass accrual.
- ❌ **Not an Algorithmic Social Feed**: No noisy social media streams, infinite scrolling, or vanity engagement bait.
- ❌ **Not a Fragile Cloud-Dependent Client**: Core logging never fails or spins indefinitely when cell signals drop in concrete basement gyms.

### 2.4 Voice & Tone Standards
- **Short, warm, direct.**
- **Verbs first.**
- **Maximum ~12 words per line of guidance.**
- **Numbers always formatted cleanly** (`tabular-nums`, rounded to nearest loadable increment).
- **Never robotic, never hype.**

| Context | What We Avoid | Vyra Tone |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Ready for workout | "Workout Session #14: Hypertrophy Split ready for execution." | "Ready when you are." |
| Short sleep / Low HRV | "Severe autonomic nervous system depression detected! Reducing prescribed tonnage by 18.5%." | "Slept 5h, so today's loads are buffered 10%." |
| Progressive overload | "Target achieved. Progressive overload algorithm indicates weight increment." | "Add 2.5 kg next time." |
| Equipment occupied | "Initiating RAG search across vector embeddings for machine alternative." | "Dumbbell incline press is a direct match." |
