# VYRA Life OS — Customer Journey Map

A progressive journey that earns complexity over time. A Week-1 user experiences an ultra-simple, ultra-fast single-pillar tool; cross-pillar intelligence unlocks naturally as habits solidify.

---

## The 5 Progressive Stages

| Stage | Trigger | Screen | Primary Action | Success Metric | Failure / Recovery State |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Day 0: Onboarding** | First app launch | Welcome $\rightarrow$ Day 0 Question | Answer: *"What do you want to master first?"* (Defaults to Strength/Train) | $\le$ 30s to Today screen; 0 drop-offs on permissions | **Recovery**: No permissions walls. If skipped, immediately default to Train and show Today's workout hero. |
| **Week 1: Daily Habit** | Notification: workout ready (max 1/day) | Today Timeline | Tap **Start** on Hero $\rightarrow$ Log workout sets ($\le$ 5s/set) | 3 completed workout logs in first 7 days | **Recovery**: Missed session? Timeline gently moves it forward without streak guilt or red warning banners. |
| **Week 2–3: Expansion** | 5th workout completed | Today Insight Card | 1-tap unlock invitation: *"You train hard. Want help dialing sleep and recovery?"* | $\ge$ 45% accept second pillar invitation | **Recovery**: User dismisses? Quietly tuck pillar into `Life` tab without re-prompting for 14 days. |
| **Month 1: The "Aha" Moment** | 15th session logged + sleep/fuel logs | Today Cross-Pillar Insight Card | Read insight: *"Your highest PRs follow 7h+ sleep and pre-workout sodium."* | User taps "Why?" or shares insight to unlock deeper coaching | **Recovery**: Insufficient correlation data? Provide training progressive overload milestones instead. |
| **Month 3+: Life Mastery** | 90 days of consistent adherence | Full Timeline + Network Room | Review periodized macro-cycle; connect with vetted gym partners | Weekly retention $\ge$ 70%; Coach proactively adapts deload weeks | **Recovery**: Overtraining detected (RIR drops below target across 3 sessions)? Coach suggests automated deload. |
