## 2. ICP Definition & The Desperate Wedge

- **Primary Persona**: 30–42 year old male tech/corporate professional (e.g. Alaa: 37yo, 173cm, 49kg baseline, sedentary desk job, Monday night recreational football, 65-min commute).
- **The Desperate Problem**: Standard bodybuilding routines take 75+ minutes and worsen desk-slouch (kyphosis/forward shoulder roll). Standard fitness apps dump 6–8 redundant exercises into a session, causing workout abandonment during 45-minute lunch windows.
- **The VYRA Wedge**: **The 34-Minute Postural Hypertrophy Architecture**. Exactly 3 slots per session. 9 working sets. Station-locked supersets requiring 0 transit across crowded gym floors.
